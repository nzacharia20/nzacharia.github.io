I am a first-year Neuroscience student at UNC Chapel Hill interested in studying the causal relationship between music therapy and depressive disorders in patients with chronic diseases. Specifically, I want to study the neurological mechanisms behind the rehabilitative approach of music therapy and how it can affect emotional processing in recovering patients. Outside academics, I perform acapella as a member of UNC Cadence, and am involved in UNC’s Red Cross chapter. In January I will join the Division of Healthcare Engineering in the UNC Hospitals’ department of Radiation Oncology as a research assistant, specifically reviewing the efficacy of supplemental AI tools in contouring prostate cancer.
